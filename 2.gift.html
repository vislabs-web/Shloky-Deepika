Dear Deepika ♡

Dear Deepika,

meri life mein aane ke liye thank you soooooooooo much.

Tumhe pata hai, tumhare aane ke baad meri life kaafi achhi aur meaningful feel hone lagi hai. Main shayad ise words mein properly explain na kar paun, but I am genuinely grateful ki tum meri life ka part ho.

Tumhari sabse special baat mere liye tumhari kind nature hai. Tum jis tarah se doosron ke saath behave karti ho, care karti ho aur chhoti-chhoti cheezon ko importance deti ho — woh mujhe genuinely bahut achha lagta hai.

Ye looks ke baare mein nahi hai. Mere liye tumhari personality, tumhari kindness aur tumhara achha dil hi tumhe itna special banata hai.

Main bas itna keh sakta hoon ki ye gentleman hamesha apni taraf se best try karega — tumhari respect karne ka, tumhe support karne ka aur ek achha insaan rehne ka.

♡

Thank you for being a beautiful part of my life.

— Shlok
